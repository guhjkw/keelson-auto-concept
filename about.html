About Keelson

About the shop

Keelson Auto Care opened in 2014 in a converted boatyard machine shop on Example Street. A keelson is the beam inside a ship's hull that keeps the whole frame true. Unglamorous, essential work: that is what we want to be for your car.

  • Harbor District
  • Six service bays
  • Hybrid and EV bay

The shop

Six bays, no sales targets

We are an independent shop, so there is no quota for extra services and no pressure to sell you anything. Our service advisors are paid the same whether you approve one line on the estimate or ten.

Our technicians work on most makes and models, from commuter sedans and family SUVs to light trucks, hybrids and EVs. When a job really does need the dealer, for example a software update only the manufacturer can install, we tell you so.

Every visit includes a digital inspection. You get photos and measurements by text, rated so you can see what is fine, what to plan for and what needs attention now.

Book a service

  1. Step 1

    Book online or call

    Choose your services and a time, and tell us whether you will wait or drop off. You get a confirmation straight away and a reminder the day before.

    About 2 minutes

  2. Step 2

    Check in with a service advisor

    We confirm what you are noticing, note the mileage and ask for your approval limit, for example "call me before anything over $200". Your keys get a numbered tag and go in a locked cabinet.

    About 10 minutes

  3. Step 3

    Inspection with photos

    A technician runs a 40-point inspection and measures what matters: brake pad thickness, tire tread, battery health. Everything worth discussing is photographed and rated good, plan soon or needs attention now.

    30 to 45 minutes

  4. Step 4

    Estimate and your approval

    You get a text link with the photos, notes and an itemized estimate, with parts and labor shown separately. Approve each line, ask questions or decline. We never start work you have not approved.

    You decide

  5. Step 5

    Repair, check and pickup

    Brake and steering work gets a second technician's check and a road test. At pickup we walk you through what we did, show you the old parts if you would like, and set your next reminder.

    Same day for most maintenance

Warranty

Covered for 24 months or 24,000 miles

Every repair we perform is covered for parts and labor, whichever limit comes first. The terms are printed on every invoice.

24/24Months / miles

One warranty, written in plain English

If a part we installed fails, or our workmanship falls short, within 24 months or 24,000 miles, we fix it at no charge. No forms to mail and no small print about labor rates.

What is covered

  • Parts we supply and install, and the labor to replace them
  • Workmanship on every repair and maintenance service
  • Our diagnosis: if the repair we recommended does not fix the problem we diagnosed, we make it right
  • Tire installation workmanship (the tires themselves carry their maker's warranty)

What is not covered

  • Normal wear on items such as pads, wipers and bulbs once they reach their expected life
  • Parts you supply yourself (our labor on them is covered for 90 days)
  • Damage from accidents, misuse, racing or neglect
  • Commercial and rideshare vehicles, which are covered for 12 months or 12,000 miles instead

How to make a claim

  • Call us, or book online and mention the warranty in your notes
  • We find your invoice by phone number, so there is no paperwork to dig out
  • More than 50 miles away? Call before any repair and we will arrange a covered repair at a shop near you
  • Warranty work is prioritized, usually the same or next business day

Shop policies

The fine print, in plain English

Short versions of the policies most shops bury in small type. If anything is unclear, ask us before you book.

Estimates and approval

We never start work without your approval. Estimates list parts and labor separately. If we find something new during a repair, we stop and ask first.

Diagnosis fees

Diagnosis starts at $99 depending on the problem. The fee is credited to the repair when you approve it within 30 days.

Customer-supplied parts

We install most customer-supplied parts at our standard labor rate. The part is not covered by our warranty, and our labor on it is covered for 90 days.

Pickup and storage

Please collect your car within three business days of our ready text. After that, storage is $25 per day. The after-hours key drop is by the office door.

Payment

Payment is due at pickup. We take major cards, contactless payments and cash. Local businesses can apply for a fleet account with monthly invoicing.

Cancellations

Reschedule or cancel any time before your appointment by phone or text, free of charge. Running late? Call us and we will fit you in.

Rides and rentals

Free ride within five miles during business hours. For longer repairs we can help you reserve a rental car near the shop.

Old parts

Ask and we will show you or return the parts we replaced, except parts that go back to the supplier for a core credit.

Your car and your data

Keys are tagged and locked away. We do not browse your phone or infotainment data, and we can clear paired phones at pickup if you ask.
