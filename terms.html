These terms show the structure a repair shop could use. Keelson Auto Care is fictional. Rules on estimates, storage fees and liens differ by state, so a real business should have its terms reviewed by a qualified professional.

What these terms cover

These terms apply to your use of the Keelson Auto Care website and online booking. Repair work itself is also covered by the repair order you approve at the shop and by the warranty described on our About page. If these terms and a signed repair order disagree, the repair order applies.

Using this website

We work to keep the information on this site accurate and current, including services, hours and starting prices, but it may occasionally be out of date. Please do not misuse the site, for example by trying to disrupt it or by submitting false bookings.

Online booking

A booking request becomes an appointment once we confirm it by text or email. We may need to reschedule if a part is delayed or a specialist technician is unavailable, and we will contact you as early as we can. You can reschedule or cancel free of charge before your appointment.

On this concept site, booking is a demo. The form validates your answers and shows a sample confirmation, but nothing is sent, stored or booked.

Prices and estimates

Prices on this website are starting-at prices for most cars and light trucks with standard parts. Your actual price depends on your vehicle, the parts you choose and what the inspection finds. Before any work begins you receive a written estimate, by text or on paper, listing parts and labor separately.

The prices shown on this concept site are sample figures for a fictional business.

Authorizing work

  • We only carry out work you approve, by signature, by text or by phone. We note how and when you approved it.
  • If we find something new during a repair, we stop and ask before doing anything outside the approved estimate.
  • You authorize us to test drive your vehicle when needed to diagnose a problem or confirm a repair.

Payment and storage

Payment is due when you collect your vehicle. We accept major cards, contactless payments and cash. Where the law allows, we may keep the vehicle until the invoice is paid.

Please collect your vehicle within three business days of our ready message. After that, storage costs $25 per day unless we have agreed otherwise.

Customer-supplied parts

We install most customer-supplied parts at our standard labor rate. We cannot warranty the part itself or labor caused by its failure, and our installation labor is covered for 90 days. We may decline parts we believe are unsafe or incorrect for your vehicle.

Warranty

Repairs we perform are covered for 24 months or 24,000 miles, whichever comes first, on parts and labor. Commercial and rideshare vehicles are covered for 12 months or 12,000 miles. Full details, exclusions and how to claim are on our warranty section.

Responsibility for your vehicle

We take care of your vehicle while it is with us and are responsible for loss or damage caused by our negligence. We are not responsible for personal items left in the vehicle, or for existing damage or conditions we note at check-in. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer protection law.
