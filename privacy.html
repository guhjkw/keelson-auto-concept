This page shows the structure and tone a repair shop privacy policy could take. Keelson Auto Care is fictional. A real business should adapt it to its own practices and have it reviewed by a qualified professional for its state or country.

Who we are

Keelson Auto Care ("Keelson", "we", "us") is an independent automotive repair and maintenance shop at 123 Example Street, Harbor District. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, book a service or bring your vehicle to the shop, and how we handle it.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: your name, mobile number and email address.
  • Vehicle details: year, make, model, mileage, VIN, license plate and service history.
  • Appointment details: the services you choose, notes you add and your preferred times.
  • Payment information when you pay at the shop. Card payments are handled by our payment processor, and we do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically

If analytics are enabled on the live site, we would collect basic usage data such as pages viewed, device type and an approximate location based on your IP address, and only after you consent where consent is required.

This concept site collects nothing. It has no analytics and no cookies, and its forms do not send data anywhere.

How we use it

  • To schedule and confirm appointments and remind you about them.
  • To diagnose, repair and maintain your vehicle, and to send inspection photos and estimates.
  • To keep service records you can use for warranty claims and resale.
  • To process payments and warranty claims.
  • To send maintenance reminders and occasional shop news if you sign up. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

Texts and email

If you give us your mobile number, we use it to send appointment confirmations, reminders, inspection reports and estimates. Reminder texts are optional. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts or HELP for help. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.

When we share it

We share information only with service providers who help us run the shop, under agreements that limit how they can use it:

  • Scheduling and messaging providers that send confirmations and reminders.
  • Payment processors that handle card transactions.
  • Parts suppliers, limited to the vehicle details needed to order the right part.
  • Manufacturers or warranty administrators, when you ask us to handle a warranty or recall claim.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, customers or staff.

Cookies and analytics

On the live site, essential cookies keep the site working. Analytics or advertising cookies, if used, would load only after you accept them in the cookie banner, and you could change your choice at any time. This concept site sets no cookies at all.

How long we keep it

We keep vehicle service records for seven years so they are available for warranty claims and resale. Booking requests that never become appointments are deleted after 12 months. Marketing contacts are removed when you unsubscribe.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop marketing messages. Depending on where you live, state privacy laws may give you additional rights. We respond within 30 days and will not treat you differently for making a request. Some records, such as invoices, must be kept for legal reasons.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including access controls on shop systems, encrypted connections on our website and locked key storage at the shop. No system is perfectly secure, so please contact us straight away if you think your information has been misused.

Children

Our services are for vehicle owners and drivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For questions or requests, contact us:
