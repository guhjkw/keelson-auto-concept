Straight answers

Questions we hear every week

Still unsure? Call +1 (555) 0100 and a service advisor will talk it through. No pressure and no upsell script.

Read our shop policies
Do I need an appointment?

Appointments get priority, but we keep time free each morning for quick checks such as warning lights, flat tires and battery tests. Booking online takes about two minutes and shows you a starting estimate as you go.

Will servicing my car here affect the factory warranty?

No. You can have scheduled maintenance done at an independent shop without voiding your new car warranty. We follow your manufacturer's schedule, use parts that meet its specifications and give you dated records to show the dealer if you ever need them.

How do estimates work?

After the inspection we text you a link with photos, notes and an itemized estimate, with parts and labor shown separately. You approve each line on its own, and we never start work you have not approved.

What does "starting at" mean on your prices?

It is the price of the job on most cars and light trucks with standard parts. Larger vehicles, premium parts or extra labor can raise it, and you see the exact number on your estimate before we begin.

Can I wait while you work?

Yes, for most services under 90 minutes. The lounge has Wi-Fi, coffee and a desk. For longer jobs we offer a free ride within five miles and text you when the car is ready.

Do you work on hybrids and electric cars?

Yes. Our hybrid and EV bay handles battery health tests, coolant service, brakes, tires, suspension and 12-volt systems. If a job truly needs the dealer, such as a software update only the manufacturer can install, we will tell you.
