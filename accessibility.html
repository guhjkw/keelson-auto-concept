Our commitment

Keelson Auto Care aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. It is the standard most often referenced for website accessibility in the United States, including in discussions of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). Accessibility is part of how this site is designed and built, not a plugin added afterwards.

What we have done

  • A "Skip to main content" link as the first thing on every page.
  • Clear page structure, with landmarks, one main heading per page and headings in a logical order.
  • Text and interface colors checked against WCAG AA contrast ratios.
  • A visible focus outline on every link, button and form field.
  • Buttons, links and form controls at least 44 by 44 pixels on touch screens.
  • A label on every form field, hints where they help, and error messages that say what to fix and link straight to the field.
  • A booking form that works with the keyboard alone, moves focus to the start of each new step and shows progress in text as well as color.
  • Service filters and the service details pop-up work by keyboard. Esc closes the pop-up and focus returns to where you were.
  • The mobile menu keeps focus inside while it is open and closes with Esc.
  • Animation is reduced or switched off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Pages reflow down to 320 pixels wide and zoom to 200 percent without losing content.
  • Decorative illustrations are hidden from screen readers, and informative ones are described in text.

How it was tested

This concept was checked with automated contrast and structure checks and with keyboard-only testing in current Chrome at phone, tablet and desktop widths. Before a real launch, the plan includes testing with NVDA on Windows and VoiceOver on iPhone and Mac, and checks in Safari, Firefox and Edge.

Known limitations

  • The location map is an illustration. Directions are written out in text next to it.
  • The booking form and newsletter sign-up are demos on this concept site and send no data.
  • On the live service, inspection photos sent by text may not include detailed descriptions. Ask and a service advisor will talk you through them.

Other ways to book

You can phone the service desk on +1 (555) 0100 during opening hours, email hello@keelson.example, or visit the front counter at 123 Example Street. The shop has step-free access, a ground floor restroom and seating in the customer lounge.

Feedback and contact

If something on this site is hard to use, please tell us which page it was on and what you were trying to do. We aim to reply within two business days and to fix confirmed problems within 30 days.
